Small Wireless Network Basics

Explore how wireless networks transmit data, the factors affecting their performance, frequency bands, and modern Wi-Fi standards.

Network Performance

  • Wireless Networks: Connect devices using radio waves instead of cables.
  • Speed vs Latency: Speed is data transfer rate; latency is the delay before data starts moving.
  • Measurement: Speed in Mbps/Gbps; Latency in milliseconds (ms).

Attenuation & Interference

  • Attenuation: Signal weakness caused by distance or physical obstacles like walls and floors.
  • Interference: Disruption from overlapping networks, microwaves, and Bluetooth.
  • Symptoms: Slower speeds, dropped connections, and fewer signal bars.

Frequency Bands

  • 2.4 GHz: Longest range, slowest speed, highly susceptible to interference.
  • 5 GHz: Medium range, faster speeds, low interference.
  • 6 GHz: Shortest range, blazing fast speeds, zero congestion.

Wi-Fi Standards

  • Progression: From 802.11n (Wi-Fi 4) to 802.11ac (Wi-Fi 5) and 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6).
  • MIMO & MU-MIMO: Utilizing multiple antennas to send data to single or multiple users simultaneously.
  • OFDMA & TWT: Channel splitting for efficiency and scheduling sleep cycles for battery saving.
