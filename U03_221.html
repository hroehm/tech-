Internal Computing Components

Explore the essential hardware inside a computer, their core purposes, and how they interact like organs in the human body.

Motherboard & BIOS

  • Motherboard: The central hub (Nervous System) connecting all components. Contains chipsets (Northbridge/Southbridge) and expansion slots.
  • BIOS/UEFI: Firmware stored on a ROM chip (Reflexes). Initiates the Power-On Self-Test (POST) and loads the OS.

Processing & Memory

  • CPU: The Brain. Executes instructions via the Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) and Control Unit (CU) using the Instruction Cycle.
  • RAM: Short-Term Memory. Volatile storage providing fast access for active programs. Uses DIMM/SODIMM modules.

Graphics & Storage

  • GPU: The Eyes. Handles parallel processing for rendering images. Can be integrated or dedicated with its own VRAM.
  • Storage: Long-Term Memory. Non-volatile hardware (HDD, SSD) that retains software, OS, and files when powered off.

Network & Cooling

  • NIC: Mouth & Ears. Enables network connections (Wired RJ-45 or Wireless 802.11). Can be onboard or expansion.
  • Cooling Systems: Temperature Control. Includes Air (fans), Liquid, and Heat Sinks to prevent thermal damage.
