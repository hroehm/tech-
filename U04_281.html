Basic Network Concepts (IoT & Connectivity)

Discover the foundational rules, identifiers, and hardware that allow computers, IoT devices, and global servers to find each other and communicate seamlessly across the internet.

Protocols & Addresses

  • Protocols: The standardized "languages" and rules devices follow to send and receive data reliably.
  • IP Address: The logical, software-assigned mailing address of a device on a network (e.g., 192.168.1.5).
  • MAC Address: The permanent, physical fingerprint burned into a device's network card at the factory.

Ports & Services

  • Ports: Virtual doorways that direct incoming data to the correct application (e.g., Port 80 for websites).
  • DNS: The internet's phonebook. Translates human-readable names (cyber.org) into IP addresses.
  • DHCP: A service that automatically leases out IP addresses to devices as they join a network.

Networking Hardware

  • Router: Directs traffic between different networks (like your home and the ISP) and performs NAT.
  • Switch: Connects devices within the same Local Area Network (LAN) using their MAC addresses.
  • Firewall: The digital bouncer that monitors and filters incoming and outgoing traffic based on security rules.

Network Types & Models

  • LAN vs WAN: Local Area Networks cover small spaces (homes). Wide Area Networks connect LANs across cities or countries.
  • Client/Server: A centralized model where powerful servers provide resources to multiple client devices.
  • Peer-to-Peer (P2P): A decentralized model where all devices are equal and share data directly with one another.
